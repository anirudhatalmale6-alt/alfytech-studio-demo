Ledgerly

Invoicing that takes ninety seconds, not an afternoon. Built for freelancers who bill in three currencies and chase payments in none of them.

The story

Ledgerly started as a spreadsheet I was maintaining for my own client work. Three currencies, a dozen active projects, and a monthly ritual of copying rows into a template that never quite lined up.

The brief I set myself was narrow on purpose: create and send an invoice in under ninety seconds, on a phone, without a manual. Everything that didn't serve that got cut — no time tracking, no CRM, no expense module.

The interesting engineering problem turned out to be offline drafts. Freelancers write invoices on trains and in cafés with bad wifi, so drafts are written to IndexedDB first and reconciled against Supabase when the connection returns. Conflicts resolve to whichever version the user touched last, and the app tells you when it does that rather than silently picking.

Payments run through Stripe Checkout so no card data ever touches my infrastructure. Reminders are a scheduled function that fires at 09:00 in the client's timezone, not mine — a small detail that measurably improved reply rates.

What it does

  • Invoice created and sent in under 90 seconds on mobile
  • Multi-currency with rates locked at issue date
  • Offline drafts synced through IndexedDB
  • Stripe Checkout — pay by card or bank transfer
  • Automatic reminders in the client's timezone
  • PDF export that matches your brand colours
  • Recurring invoices with a one-tap pause
  • Tax summary export for your accountant
